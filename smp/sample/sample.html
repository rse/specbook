SPEC: Data Model (DM) ⚓︎

ENTITY: Unit ⚓︎

An organizational unit of the enterprise — BECAUSE the organization is structured into nested units which group the people working in them.

ATTRIBUTETYPEDEFAULTDescription
id ⚓︎unique uuiduuid()

Unique identifier of the unit — BECAUSE every unit needs a stable handle independent of its name

name ⚓︎string

Full name of the unit such as “Research and Development” — BECAUSE the unit is addressed by a human-readable label

abbreviation ⚓︎string

Short form of the unit name such as “R&D” — BECAUSE compact renderings like org charts have no room for the full name

RELATIONTYPEARITYDescription
parentUnit ⚓︎ENTITY: Unit ⚭0..1

The unit this unit is nested in — BECAUSE the units form a hierarchy whose top-most unit has no parent

members ⚓︎ENTITY: Person ⚭*

The people assigned to this unit — BECAUSE a unit is staffed by an arbitrary number of people

director ⚓︎ENTITY: Person ⚭0..1

The person heading this unit — BECAUSE a unit has at most one accountable lead

ENTITY: Person ⚓︎

An individual person working in the organization — BECAUSE the organization is staffed by people who are assigned to its units.

ATTRIBUTETYPEDEFAULTDescription
id ⚓︎unique uuiduuid()

Unique identifier of the person — BECAUSE names are neither stable nor unique across the organization

name ⚓︎string

Full name of the person such as “Jane Doe” — BECAUSE people are addressed by their human-readable name

initials ⚓︎string

Short form of the person name such as “JD” — BECAUSE compact renderings like org charts have no room for the full name

role ⚓︎string

Job role of the person such as “Software Engineer” — BECAUSE the responsibility of a person has to be visible beside their unit assignment

RELATIONTYPEARITYDescription
belongsTo ⚓︎ENTITY: Unit ⚭0..1

The unit this person is assigned to — BECAUSE a person works in at most one unit at a time

supervisor ⚓︎ENTITY: Person ⚭0..1

The person this person reports to — BECAUSE the reporting lines form a hierarchy whose top-most person has no supervisor
