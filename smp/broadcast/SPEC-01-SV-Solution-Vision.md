#   SPEC: Solution Vision (SV)

##  ASPECT: Purpose {{purpose}}

Broadcast exists to let organizations stream live video events to
thousands of authenticated attendees while keeping the audience engaged
through moderated chat, questions, and likes, all under full control of
who may watch and interact.

##  ASPECT: Target Audience {{target-audience}}

The solution serves event organizers (Managers), live-event moderators
and presenters, and large invited audiences of attendees, ranging from
small internal lectures of 75 people up to digital townhalls of 10000
simultaneous participants.

##  ASPECT: Value Proposition {{value-proposition}}

Broadcast delivers a privacy-by-design, GDPR-compliant,
self-operated streaming platform that combines email-verified access
control, streaming-provider independence, and real-time moderated
interaction in a single browser-based product.

##  ASPECT: Differentiators {{differentiators}}

Unlike generic streaming platforms, Broadcast enforces per-attendee
access limits, switches streaming providers live during an event without
user interaction, anonymizes all personal data on event finish, and runs
cost-efficiently on self-hosted European infrastructure.

##  ASPECT: Future State {{future-state}}

Once adopted, Broadcast replaces third-party event platforms as the
organization's primary channel for large-scale live events, providing
predictable per-event cost, full data sovereignty, and a consistent,
engaging attendee experience.
