SPEC: Solution Vision (SV)
==========================

-   PURPOSE:
    **Broadcast** exists to let organizations stream live video events to
    thousands of attendees while keeping the audience engaged through
    moderated chat, questions, and likes, all under full control of who
    may watch and interact.

-   TARGET-AUDIENCE:
    The solution serves event organizers (managers), live-event
    moderators and presenters, and large invited audiences of attendees,
    ranging from small internal lectures of 75 people up to digital town
    halls of 10.000 simultaneous attendees.

-   SOLUTION:
    **Broadcast** is a live-event platform that joins an embedded video
    player of an external streaming provider with an email-verified
    access control and a moderated interaction channel in a single web
    application.

-   VALUE-PROPOSITION:
    Organizers keep full control over who watches and who may interact,
    and receive all attendee input in a form they can moderate,
    forward to the presenter, and export afterwards. Attendees reach
    the event through a single personal link in any recent browser,
    without installation or accounts, and see their questions reach the
    presenters within seconds.

-   DIFFERENTIATORS:
    Compared to public streaming portals, **Broadcast** admits only
    invited (and optionally authenticated) attendees and enforces
    per-attendee access limits. It stays independent of any single
    streaming provider and switches providers live during a running
    event without user interaction. Compared to the status quo of
    renting a third-party event platform per event, it runs on
    self-operated infrastructure, keeps all personal data under the
    organization's own GDPR responsibility, and anonymizes it on event
    finish.

-   SCOPE:
    **Broadcast** covers anonymous and authenticated event access,
    video playback, moderated interaction, and event statistics. It
    deliberately neither produces nor encodes nor hosts the video
    streams itself -- ingest and delivery stay with the external
    streaming providers -- and it is neither a conferencing tool with
    attendee audio and video, nor a video-on-demand archive.

-   SUCCESS-CRITERIA:
    **Broadcast** is achieved once an organization can run its
    large-scale events on it end-to-end: events of up to 10.000
    simultaneous attendees are carried without a stream interruption
    visible to the audience.

-   FUTURE-STATE:
    Once adopted, live events are no longer bought per event from
    external platforms but can be held on an organization's own channel:
    the attendee data never leaves its control, the streaming provider
    becomes an exchangeable supplier instead of the platform, and
    organizers reuse one familiar event setup instead of a different
    tool for every occasion.
