SPEC: Solution Vision (SV)
==========================

-   PURPOSE:
    Broadcast exists to let organizations stream live video events to thousands of authenticated
    attendees while keeping the audience engaged through moderated chat, questions, and likes, all
    under full control of who may watch and interact.

-   TARGET-AUDIENCE:
    The solution serves event organizers (Managers), live-event moderators and presenters, and large
    invited audiences of attendees, ranging from small internal lectures of 75 people up to digital
    town halls of 10000 simultaneous participants.

-   VALUE-PROPOSITION:
    Broadcast delivers a privacy-by-design, GDPR-compliant, self-operated streaming platform that
    combines email-verified access control, streaming-provider independence, and real-time moderated
    interaction in a single browser-based product.

-   DIFFERENTIATORS:
    Unlike generic streaming platforms, Broadcast enforces per-attendee access limits, switches
    streaming providers live during an event without user interaction, anonymizes all personal data
    on event finish, and runs cost-efficiently on self-hosted European infrastructure.

-   FUTURE-STATE:
    Once adopted, Broadcast replaces third-party event platforms as the organization's primary
    channel for large-scale live events, providing predictable per-event cost, full data
    sovereignty, and a consistent, engaging attendee experience.
