This handbook plans the delivery of **Broadcast**, a Web application
complementing a broadcasted video stream with a chat backchannel, stream
selection, and live voting for enterprise-grade "townhall" events. It states
the objectives, constraints, and assumptions justifying the project and the
budget funding it, and names the parties and persons delivering the solution,
the results they hand over, the work breakdown of the scope, the schedule of
the work with its milestones, the risks threatening and the opportunities
favoring the delivery, the ceremonies steering it, and the issues and changes
it handled so far.

The handbook is a sample of the *project management* schema configuration of
SpecBook, standing on its own beside the specification of the solution: the
specification describes *what* is built, the handbook *who* builds it, *when*,
and *how* the project is steered.
