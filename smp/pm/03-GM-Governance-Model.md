#   PM: Governance Model (GM)

##  CEREMONY: Steering Committee {{steering}}

-   KIND:         Meeting
-   CADENCE:      Monthly
-   PARTICIPANTS: [[maria]], [[tom]], [[anna]], [[lukas]]

The steering committee decides on the budget, the scope changes, and the risk
responses beyond the authority of the project manager, BECAUSE the sponsor
owns the budget and the product owner owns the scope.

##  CEREMONY: Status Report {{status}}

-   KIND:         Report
-   CADENCE:      Weekly
-   PARTICIPANTS: [[anna]], [[tom]], [[maria]]

The status report tells the progress against the plan, the budget consumption,
and the open risks in one page, BECAUSE the sponsor and the product owner steer
on facts, not on impressions.

##  CEREMONY: Stakeholder Update {{update}}

-   KIND:         Report
-   CADENCE:      Monthly
-   PARTICIPANTS: [[anna]], [[acme]], [[nova]], [[hetzner]], [[workforce]]

The stakeholder update informs all parties on the milestones reached, the
next ones, and the decisions concerning them, and collects the questions of
the workforce on the backchannel, BECAUSE the parties without a seat in the
steering committee still have to plan around the project.

##  CEREMONY: Sprint Review {{sprint-review}}

-   KIND:         Review
-   CADENCE:      Bi-weekly
-   PARTICIPANTS: [[tom]], [[anna]], [[sara]], [[jonas]], [[lea]]

The sprint review demonstrates the increment of the last two weeks to the
product owner and collects the findings, BECAUSE early feedback is cheaper
than late change requests.

##  CEREMONY: Daily Stand-up {{standup}}

-   KIND:         Meeting
-   CADENCE:      Daily
-   PARTICIPANTS: [[anna]], [[lukas]], [[sara]], [[jonas]], [[lea]]

The stand-up synchronizes the team on the work of the day and the impediments,
BECAUSE the parallel work streams of the construction phase depend on each
other.

##  CEREMONY: Phase Gate {{gate}}

-   KIND:         Gate
-   CADENCE:      At milestone
-   PARTICIPANTS: [[maria]], [[tom]], [[anna]]

The phase gate takes the go/no-go decision at the end of every phase on the
acceptance of its deliverables, BECAUSE the next phase commits budget the
sponsor releases only on accepted results.
